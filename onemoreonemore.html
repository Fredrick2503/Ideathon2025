About the event

A national platform for real ideas

Welcome to the National-Level AICTE IDEA LAB Ideathon at New Horizon College of Engineering! This dynamic platform brings together innovative minds from across the country to address real-world challenges.

It's your opportunity to collaborate with students nationwide, transform ideas into impactful solutions, and showcase your innovation to industry experts, mentors, and academicians.

The Ideathon goes beyond competition; it is a national platform for aspiring innovators to connect, create, and turn promising ideas into solutions with meaningful impact.

Embark on a transformative national journey to build, learn, and launch the next big idea — and be part of shaping the future through innovation and collaboration.

Before you register

Rules & guidelines

  • Team size: Teams can consist of 1 to 6 members. You can register as a solo innovator or as a team.
  • Interdisciplinary teams: We highly encourage forming teams with students from different branches of study.
  • Cross-college teams: Teams must be formed within the same college; inter-college teams are not allowed.
  • Originality: Your idea must be your own work, developed for this event.
  • Respect: Treat all participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect.
  • Judges' decision is final.
