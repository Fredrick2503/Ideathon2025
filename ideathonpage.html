Ideathon 2025

AICTE IDEA LAB @ NHCE

Collaborate • Innovate • Launch Groundbreaking Solutions

About the Event

Welcome to the AICTE IDEA LAB Ideathonat New Horizon College of Engineering! This dynamic event brings together bright, creative minds to solve real-world challenges.

It's your chance to collaborate, innovate, and launch groundbreaking solutions. Join us to build teams, develop visions, and showcase your talent to experts.

The Ideathon is more than a competition; it's a vibrant space where aspiring innovators can truly shine.

Embark on a transformative journey to build, learn, and launch the next big thing. Be part of shaping the future through innovation and collaboration!

Tracks & Problem Statements

Disaster Management

  • PS-1A: AI-Driven Fake News Filter
  • PS-1B: Dynamic Evacuation Routing System
  • PS-1C: Digital System for Missing Persons
  • PS-1D: Crowd Stampede Prevention
  • PS-1E: Blockchain for Aid Transparency

Fintech (Financial Technology)

  • PS-2A: Inclusive Digital Credit Assessment
  • PS-2B: AI-Enabled Fraud Detection in UPI
  • PS-2C: Voice-First Banking for Rural Users
  • PS-2D: Gamified Financial Literacy Platform for Gen Z
  • PS-2E: Cross-Border Tuition Payment System

Sustainable Energy Management

  • PS-3A: Smart Campus Energy Management
  • PS-3B: Affordable Solar Energy Storage
  • PS-3C: Community Waste-to-Energy Solution
  • PS-3D: Green Finance Tracking Application
  • PS-3E: Vertical Axis Wind Turbines for Cities

Space Tech

  • PS-4A: Remote Sensing for Precision Agriculture
  • PS-4B: NAVIC-Based Emergency Services
  • PS-4C: Geospatial Data for Urban Planning
  • PS-4D: Early Warning System for Cloudbursts
  • PS-4E: Space Debris Mitigation Strategy

Smart Vehicles & Transportation

  • PS-5A: AI for Traffic Flow Optimization
  • PS-5B: Smart Parking Solution
  • PS-5C: Intelligent EV Charging Network
  • PS-5D: Affordable EV Retrofit Kits
  • PS-5E: Smart Driver Assistance for Accident Prevention

Rules & Guidelines

  • Team size: Teams can consist of 1 to 6 members. You can register as a solo innovator or as a team.
  • Interdisciplinary Teams: We highly encourage forming teams with students from different branches of study.
  • Originality: Your idea must be your own work, developed for this event.
  • Respect: Treat all participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect.
  • Judges' decision is final

Registration Information

Registration Fee

A non-refundable registration fee of ₹300 per team is required to confirm your spot.

Registration Deadline

The deadline for registration is November 15, 2025. All teams must register online before this date.

Required Documents

All participants must carry their valid college ID card for verification at the venue on the day of the event.
